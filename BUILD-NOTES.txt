Toni's Total Training Tracker v49 - local preview build

Each scheduled marathon walk/run session can have its own audio class selected from the saved Class Library. The selector appears on that date's marathon card in LOG. At a Glance presents Marathon walk/run as a distinct row and shows its audio class beneath it, separate from other cardio activities. A matching Walk/Run class already associated with that date's walk is recognized for the marathon row. Elliptical and other shared cardio-library entries no longer appear on non-cardio day types.

This is a local review build only. The live Netlify site has not been changed or deployed. Existing storage keys and saved exercise measurements are preserved.
