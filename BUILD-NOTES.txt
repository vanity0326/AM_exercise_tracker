Toni's Total Training Tracker v50 - weekly At a Glance

The app opens on a Monday-through-Sunday At a Glance. Week navigation and Choose week let the user review other weeks; Print opens the browser's print dialog. Each day displays its selected plan category, cardio activities and assigned class names, the marathon walk/run prescription and its separate class, and the alternating Achilles PT routine with dosage. Strength exercise lists, quotes, recovery, category totals, completion tracking, and column headings are omitted from this overview as requested.

The overview reads existing planner, activity, class-library, and marathon data without changing workout history or storage keys. At a Glance remains read-only. Existing app features and saved data are preserved. A local-only sample week is provided when opening the files directly with no local tracker data; it is never written to storage and is not used by the deployed HTTPS app.

This is a local review build only. The live Netlify site has not been changed or deployed.
