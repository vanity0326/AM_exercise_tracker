Toni's Total Training Tracker v43

Deployment
1. In Netlify, open the existing Exercise Planner site and choose Deploy manually.
2. Upload the contents of this ZIP so index.html is at the publish root (do not upload the ZIP as a nested folder).
3. After deploy, open index.html and use the Achilles Rehab button near the top. It opens achilles.html.

The Achilles Rehab link is highlighted in teal near the top of the tracker, and the rehab page is included in the offline cache. Tracker and rehab saved-data keys and storage logic are unchanged. This ZIP does not deploy itself.
